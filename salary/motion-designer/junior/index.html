How these figures are made

Every number is annual fixed CTC in ₹ lakh per annum. These are research estimates, drawn from public job listings, salary aggregators and published Indian design and startup compensation reports. They are not yet based on salaries submitted on Design Wallet. Community submissions replace the research figure for a role, level and city once at least 5 people have reported it. Use these as a starting point for a negotiation, not a quote.
