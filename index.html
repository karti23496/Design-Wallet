Yes. The catalogue, salary data, mini tools and the account itself are all free.

Do I need an account to browse?

Yes. The catalogue, salary data, Wall of Portfolios and Wallet Reads open once you’re signed in. The account is free and takes a minute.

Can I sign up with Google?

Yes. Choose Continue with Google, or sign up with your name, email and a password.

What do you do with my email?

We use it for your account: confirming your address and resetting your password. Newsletters only go out if you tick the box when you set up your account, and you can unsubscribe at any time.
